About

I am a PhD student in Automation at Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Gao Huang and Prof. Shiji Song.

Previously, I obtained an MSc in Computer Science from King’s College London.

My research interests center on embodied intelligence, robot learning, and robotic manipulation.
